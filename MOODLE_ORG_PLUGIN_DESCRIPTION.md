<p>Admissions and the families who come in, for any school: a school, a nursery, an academy or a vocational training centre. Families who ask for information or a place feel welcomed by people, without an account; the team sees in a minute which households are waiting, how many places each course still has, and why pupils come and go all year round.</p>
<p><strong>The first day</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>To open admissions</strong> at the top of the inbox, in six steps (the offer and its places, who receives the families, the address and contact, the first open day, the ways families come in and how a place is applied for), each ticked when it is really done.</li>
<li><strong>How is it done?</strong> answers as you type, forgives typos and knows the centre's settings. <strong>See it step by step</strong> plays a guided tour with voice on a practice school of its own, where nothing is ever sent. Printable guides at <a href="https://eguahe.com/guias/guia-empezar-en.pdf">eguahe.com</a>.</li>
<li>Each function introduces itself in the inbox when its time comes: the social media kit of an open day, the common family record with the first enrolment, the offer of next school year before asking who goes on.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>For families, without an account</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Sign-up for an open day, visits in turns or a visit of their own, with an antispam check that asks nothing of them, from a link, a QR code on a poster or a form embedded in the school's website.</li>
<li>A page of their own for each household: their visit with its calendar file, who receives them with their photo, how to get there on an illustrated map of the school with its little guide dog, what they told, and how to apply for a place. They download or erase their data with one button.</li>
<li>Emails at each moment (confirmation, reminder, a change, thanks after the visit) and, optionally, the same notices by WhatsApp or Telegram.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>The team's desk</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>The inbox</strong>, the family waiting longest first, and <strong>the record of each household</strong>: its children and their stage (inquiry, visit, application, place, enrolled), and the whole conversation, whatever way it came: the website form, Google Forms, Meta lead ads, an admissions mailbox, a phone call, the door of an open day.</li>
<li><strong>Answering</strong> with the greeting in place, what they asked ticked off as it is answered, promises with their day, usual answers, and who looks after each family. With the site's AI, a draft that a person reads, changes and sends.</li>
<li><strong>Open days</strong> with capacity and turns, posters, backpack labels with each child's name, the door on the day, «How it went» afterwards, and a kit for the school's social media.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Numbers that do not mislead</strong></p>
<p>The way to enrolment, never sign-ups alone; the net balance of new pupils and leavers through the year; first response time by channel; where families come from and the cost per new pupil; weekly and end-of-year reports; and a forecast of the next three years from open birth data, always aggregated. Eguahe never gives a family a score, a probability or a ranking.</p>
<p><strong>After enrolment</strong></p>
<p>Who goes on next school year, answered by each family on its page; facts that tell a family may leave, with their day; brothers and sisters for later years; families who offer to tell others how it went; and, for the youngest, the illustrated story «Your first day», with their name and their drawing (its art comes in a separate free package).</p>
<p><strong>Configuration and privacy</strong></p>
<p>Site administration → Plugins → Local plugins → Admissions (Oksigenia Eguahe); the rest from the school's own settings. Covered by the Privacy API; families who stop answering are erased after the period each school sets. Everything stays in your Moodle; outside services only when the school asks for them (open data, the positions of places, a link shortener). The AI goes through Moodle's AI subsystem, with the provider and key of each site.</p>
<p><strong>Works with</strong></p>
<p>Optional, each on its own: Oksigenia Family, the common record of families shared with other plugins; Oksigenia Marandu, for WhatsApp and Telegram; and Oksigenia Aty, the mobile app of a hosted school.</p>
<p><strong>Languages and requirements</strong></p>
<p>English, Spanish and Portuguese (Portugal and Brazil); the families' pages also in Arabic, right to left. Moodle 4.5 to 5.3, any theme, light and dark. Tested with PHPUnit in CI on every branch from 4.5 to 5.3. GPL v3 or later; bundled libraries in thirdpartylibs.xml.</p>
